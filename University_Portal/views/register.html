Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

CUSIT University is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use the Smart Campus Portal.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information during registration and usage: Full Name, University Email Address, Department, Password (stored securely using bcrypt hashing), Complaints and event registration activity, and FYP group participation data.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to: (a) authenticate your identity and manage your portal access, (b) process and track complaints, (c) manage event registrations, (d) coordinate FYP group activities, and (e) deliver campus-wide announcements.

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your data is only accessible to authorized administrators within CUSIT University for the purpose of portal management.

Your data is retained for the duration of your enrollment at CUSIT University. Upon graduation or account deletion, your personal data will be removed from the system within 90 days.
